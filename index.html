My Theme

I liked the ChatGPT Modern BI best, but I also wanted a red. So I removed the Dark Navy from the Modern BI theme and inserted the Dark Orange from the Claude theme in position 4 in the Modern BI theme. The result looks very similar to the Power BI built in 'Classroom' theme.
